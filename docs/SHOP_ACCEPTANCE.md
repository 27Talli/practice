# Shop deployment acceptance

Use Demo and synthetic products first. These checks require the actual phone, labels, printer and owner-held signing key; emulator results do not replace them.

1. Record Android version, app version and signing certificate. Store the permanent release key/passwords privately with a second offline copy. Confirm an update signed with that same key preserves data. Never uninstall or clear an existing business installation to resolve a certificate mismatch.
2. Enable airplane mode. Open Offer Point, create a test product with cost/selling price/stock and a manufacturer barcode using Scan existing barcode in the product form. Check the captured value, including leading zeros, before Save. Test scanning while editing too, cancel capture and deny camera access to confirm manual entry remains usable. Restart the app; verify it remains saved. Check Fancy Footwears sees its own stock/history only.
3. Print a registered Code 128 cost label at the intended physical size. Verify there is no plain cost/key on the label. Scan both manufacturer and internal labels in normal shop light using the rear camera. Check leading-zero barcodes, a held code, Scan next item, an unknown code, and denied camera permission/manual lookup. The coded cost is a staff convention, not cryptographic secrecy.
4. Sell one item for exact cash, one with change, and one with both discounts. Repeated taps must produce one sale and one stock deduction. Restart, reopen history and check original snapshots after a product price/cost change. Verify a cart cannot oversell current stock. Open the staff icon to check current selling/purchase prices, positive/negative gross profit and discounted totals; Hide it before presenting the phone. Check Android blocks a screenshot of that private dialog. Its price scanner must not add to the checkout cart.
5. Reprint/share the same sale. Inspect A4/58/80 mm PDFs; ensure totals, receipt number and QR are readable and no purchase cost/profit/cost code is present. The QR must decode to exactly the printed receipt number. For direct printing pair/select the actual supported Bluetooth Classic SPP or USB printer-class ESC/POS device. Grant its permission; print the explicit no-sale test. Check width, wrapping, large receipts and cutter if enabled. Disconnect/reconnect it; check partial paper output before a retry. Record the printer make/model/connection and any required model-specific driver.
6. Open Returns & refunds and scan the original receipt QR; also try typing its number. Check wrong-shop and unknown receipts are rejected and scanning alone never posts a refund. Checkout and saved sale must have no refund action. Return part of a discounted sale, restock some units and mark others unsaleable. Verify the refund shown and actual cash handed back, refund reference/QR, original-sale link, remaining return quantity and physical stock. Return the remainder; combined refunds must equal the original amount paid for those goods. Reprinting a refund must not change balances.
7. Add real-shaped test expenses in each shop, void an incorrect one with a reason, and compare today's and custom-date reports with hand-calculated sale/refund/expense totals. Profit uses original recorded sale costs; damaged returned units retain their cost. Cash movement is not a counted till balance or complete accounting ledger. Review CSV privately.
8. Set the local PIN, lock/unlock with an unpaid cart and restart the app. Check wrong-attempt delays, chosen inactivity timeout and device-credential recovery. PIN changes never print/store a plain PIN; portable business backups exclude this device credential.
9. Create an encrypted Demo backup in the actual chosen Android document provider. Copy it off the phone, reopen it with the correct passphrase and inspect counts. Reject a wrong passphrase. Add another test record, restore the earlier backup using a new recovery destination and typed confirmation, and then inspect/restore the recovery copy. Check both shops, label aliases, sales/refunds/expenses, alert levels and receipts. A business restore retains this phone's PIN and clears unpaid carts.
10. Repeat app startup without connectivity and after an ordinary phone restart. Confirm camera and printer permissions are requested only for their relevant actions. Confirm the phone clock/timezone is correct (business dates are Asia/Karachi).
11. Install a verified retained-key Live release only after the above passes. Live begins without Demo products or sales. Enter actual opening stock, costs and prices and count them against shelves. Keep real shop data out of GitHub, CI fixtures and support screenshots.
12. Agree a daily closing routine: compare cash activity and expenses, count actual till cash separately, export encrypted backups, keep off-device copies and test recovery periodically. Keep the passphrase and retained signing key independently secure.

Record date, phone, printer, app source/run, signing certificate and the result of each check. If hardware is unsupported, continue using PDF/Android printing or integrate the supplied model before relying on direct receipt output. Do not treat a successful Bluetooth/USB transfer as proof that paper printed.
